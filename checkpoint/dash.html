Add students

Students on your roster only type the Session ID; their name and period come from here. Paste one student per line: school email, first name or initials, class period. You can copy the three columns straight from a spreadsheet. Each email is turned into the student’s private code right away and is never saved.

Students who signed in to one of your sessions can also be added from My sessions with Add to roster.
