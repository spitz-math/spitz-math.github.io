DIG BUDS

Slope-intercept form. Six little Buds are lost under your island. You have 5 minutes to dig down and bring them home.

1. AnswerPress Q for questions. Each right answer charges your energy and earns cash.
2. DigWalk to a glowing beam and hold X to dig straight down. A lost Bud is waiting right under every beam.
3. RescueOre pays cash, crystals give energy. Spend cash in the shop (B) on a Drill, an Ore Magnet and more.
