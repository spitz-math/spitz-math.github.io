# Art brief: slingshot for the Burger Blaster title painting

Status: written 2026-11-27, NOT sent. One try only. Kody approves the exact file at dev.spitzmath.com/approve/ before it is used.

## Why
The title painting still shows the first slingshot (small, red rubber bands). The game now uses an orange wooden slingshot with flat leather straps. Only the slingshot gets painted. Claude cuts it out, shrinks it and sets it on the rooftop in the title painting, so the rest of the approved painting is untouched.

## Brief (paste as is, with the one style picture attached)
Use image generation. Paint ONE object: a wooden Y-shaped slingshot.

- Camera: side view with a slight three-quarter turn toward the viewer, at eye height. The slingshot stands upright and leans a little to the left. The front prong is nearer and a bit thicker. The back prong sits behind it, a little to the right and a little thinner.
- Wood: warm crate orange with darker orange grain lines, chunky and hand-carved.
- Straps: two flat brown leather straps, not rubber. Each strap is tucked under a wrap of darker leather cord at the top of its prong. The back strap runs behind the fork and the front strap runs in front of it.
- Pouch: a small brown leather pouch joins the two straps, pulled back to the left and slightly down, empty.
- Style: match the attached picture. Thick dark brown ink outline, flat cel shading with one shadow tone, light halftone dots in the shadows, matte.
- Background: plain flat white, nothing else in the picture. Large size, about 1500 px tall, the whole slingshot inside the frame with clear space around it.

No gloss, no 3D render look, no rubber bands, no burger, no hands, no ground, no text.
