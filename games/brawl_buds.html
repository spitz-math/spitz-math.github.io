BRAWL BUDS

Slope-intercept form. A 4-minute brawl against the Rivals. Answers, hits and KOs score points, plus 3 challenges every brawl.

1. CornerBefore every round, answer questions. Right answers charge your Supers. Wrong answers charge nothing, so don’t guess.
2. Fight← → move. Space jumps (twice in the air). J hits and K throws a Super. Hold ↑ or ↓ while you hit for other moves.
3. Knock outHits raise the Rival’s %, so they fly farther. Grab the items that drop: bat, bomb, spring shoes, burger. Most KOs in 4 minutes wins.
Keys: ← → or A D move · Space, ↑ or W jump · ↓ or S drop down · J or Z hit · K or X Super · Enter corner
